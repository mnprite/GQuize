Part 1 : [Can, can be, can have]

> Can 
1. Ability (सकता , पता )
(i) present = can/ be able to [be = is/am/are]
(ii)past = could/ be able to [be = was/were]
(iii)future= will be able to 

Q. Ability ➔ present 
answer : can, is able to, am able to, are able to (these are four answers of this question)
Q. Ability ➔ Past ? 
answers : could, was able to, were able to (these are three answers of this question)
Q. Ability ➔ Future ?
answer : will be able to.


2. Possibiliy (सकता)
can, coul, may, might
Q. Possibility ?
answer : can , could, may, might (these are four answers of this question)


3. Request (सकता)
can, could, may
Q. Request  ?
answer : can, could, may (these are three answers of this question) 

Permission (सकता)
can, could, may
Q. Permission ?
answer : can, could, may (these are three answers of this question)
