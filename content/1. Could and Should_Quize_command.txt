RULE 1 - Ignore uppercase/lowercase and spacing.
Capital or small letters never matter. "COULD BE", "Could be", "could be" are all the same. Ignore extra spaces. RULE 7 - Exact phrase match only.

Ignore only a trailing grammar note such as "+ V1", "+ V3", "+ verb" (example: "should have + V3" counts as "should have").

RULE 8 - Apply these rules to every question, without exception.

RULE 9 - Automatically ask the next question.
